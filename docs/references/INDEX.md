# Seed library index

Verified metadata, BibTeX (`library.bib`), extracted text (`text/`) and PDFs (`pdfs/`, files over 5 MB omitted; text kept) for foundational and recent AI/LLM/SWE papers. Every entry was retrieved from arXiv and/or Crossref by `scripts/fetch_source.py`; `registry.json` holds the full record, including the abstract and retrieval date.

**Tier column.** `peer-reviewed` = a Crossref journal/proceedings record with a publisher DOI matching title and first author. `preprint` = *a published version was not confirmed by the tool*; many of these appeared at venues such as NeurIPS, ICLR, ICML or ACL. Confirm on the publisher or proceedings page and record it with `fetch_source.py set-venue KEY "Venue Year" --url <page>` before describing one as peer-reviewed.

**Use.** This is a starting corpus of well-known anchors, not coverage of any specific niche; search for the topic at hand. Before attributing a number or finding to an entry, check it: `python scripts/check_claim.py KEY "phrase"`.
